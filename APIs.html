Free APIs (no key needed)


Live feeds

Their data changes on its own, every few minutes or, for some, once a day.

  • Where the ISS Is: position of the International Space Station
  • Sunrise Sunset: sunrise and sunset times for any place
  • Open-Meteo: weather forecasts and current conditions, anywhere
  • Open-Meteo Air Quality: air pollution levels
  • National Weather Service: US forecasts and alerts
  • wttr.in: weather in one simple request
  • USGS Earthquakes: earthquakes worldwide, updated every minute
  • NOAA Aurora Forecast: where the northern lights are likely right now
  • Launch Library 2: upcoming rocket launches with countdowns and images
  • NOAA Tides & Currents: water levels, including at the Battery in Manhattan
  • UK Carbon Intensity: how clean the UK's electricity is right now
  • iNaturalist: plant and animal sightings posted by people
  • Citi Bike: bikes and docks at every station
  • Amtraker: live positions of every Amtrak train
  • Digitraffic Marine: live positions of ships around Finland and the Baltic Sea
  • Digitraffic Weathercam: hundreds of Finnish road cameras, with a new photo every few minutes
  • NYC Open Data: 311 complaints, street trees, restaurant inspections and more
  • Chicago Data Portal: Chicago city data
  • Transport for London: London Underground status
  • Wikimedia Feed: today's featured article, picture of the day, on this day
  • Wikipedia Pageviews: the most-read articles each day
  • Wikipedia Live Edits: every Wikipedia edit as it happens (read it with EventSource instead of fetch)
  • Lichess: puzzle of the day, games and players
  • TVmaze: TV shows and what's airing today
  • Frankfurter: exchange rates, updated every working day
  • Currency API: exchange rates, including crypto

Other APIs

These aren't live: they return the same data every time, or something random. You can still connect them to a live feed, for example the weather from Open-Meteo with photos from Lorem Picsum.

Space, weather & earth

  • Open-Meteo Geocoding: city name to latitude and longitude
  • NASA Image Library: NASA photos, video and audio

Nature

  • GBIF: millions of species records and museum specimens
  • Dog CEO: dog photos by breed
  • RandomFox: fox photos
  • Cat Facts and Meow Facts: cat facts

Places & time

  • REST Countries: flags, capitals, population and languages
  • Zippopotam.us: zip codes to places
  • Nager.Date: public holidays in every country
  • ipapi: the visitor's approximate location

Art, books & words

  • Art Institute of Chicago: artworks with images
  • The Met: the Metropolitan Museum's collection, including fashion from the Costume Institute (search with /v1.1/search, the old /v1/search no longer works)
  • V&A: the Victoria and Albert Museum's collection, with lots of fashion and textiles
  • Cleveland Museum of Art: open-access artworks
  • Rijksmuseum: the Dutch national museum's collection
  • Open Library: books and covers
  • PoetryDB: poems by author and title
  • Datamuse: rhymes, synonyms and related words
  • Wikipedia: article summaries and images
  • Advice Slip: random advice
  • Bible API: Bible verses

Games & fandoms

  • PokéAPI: Pokémon
  • Deck of Cards: shuffle and draw cards, with card images
  • Open Trivia DB: trivia questions
  • D&D 5e: Dungeons & Dragons monsters, spells and classes
  • Scryfall: Magic: The Gathering cards
  • Studio Ghibli: films and characters
  • Harry Potter: characters and spells
  • Disney: Disney characters
  • Chess.com: player profiles and games

Jokes, quotes & oddities

  • Official Joke API, JokeAPI, icanhazdadjoke, Chuck Norris: jokes
  • Kanye Rest, Taylor Swift, Ron Swanson: quotes
  • yesno.wtf: yes or no, with a GIF
  • Useless Facts: random facts
  • Corporate BS Generator: random business buzzwords

Images & color

  • Lorem Picsum: placeholder photos
  • DiceBear: generated avatars
  • RoboHash: robot avatars from any text
  • QR Code Generator: QR codes as images
  • The Color API: color names and color schemes
  • EmojiHub: emoji by category

Music, TV & food

  • iTunes Search: songs, 30-second previews and album art
  • Radio Browser: live radio stations worldwide
  • lyrics.ovh: song lyrics
  • TheMealDB: recipes with photos
  • TheCocktailDB: drinks with photos
  • Open Food Facts: food products by barcode
  • Open Brewery DB: breweries
